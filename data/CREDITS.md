# Data credits & licenses

## `countryStats.json`

`countryStats.json` is a derived dataset bundled with the app. It powers
Higher/Lower mode and the country-facts info modal. It is regenerated by
[`scripts/gen-country-stats.cjs`](../scripts/gen-country-stats.cjs).

It combines two open sources:

| Field(s) | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| names, capital, area, currency, region, subregion | [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries) | [Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/) |
| population | [World Bank — SP.POP.TOTL](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

Population figures are the latest available from the World Bank (**2024** values).
Snapshot last compiled **June 2026**.

A few entries the upstream sources don't cover are filled manually in the
generator: the four UK home nations (England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland)
and the population of Kosovo, Taiwan, Vatican City, and Antarctica.

### License notes

- **ODbL (mledoze/countries):** `countryStats.json` is a derivative database, so
  it is offered under the ODbL — keep this attribution with it. Works that merely
  *display* the data (the app UI) only require attribution, which is shown in the
  app footer and the info modal.
- **CC BY 4.0 (World Bank):** requires attribution; provided here and in-app.
- **Flags** (the SVG images in `public/flags/`) are **not** covered by these
  licenses and are credited separately (Flagpedia) — national flags / emblems
  have their own copyright status.

## `countryCentroids.json`

`countryCentroids.json` is a derived dataset bundled with the app: one
`[longitude, latitude]` pair per country, used by Daily Deduction to work out the
bearing and distance between a guess and the answer. It is regenerated by
[`scripts/gen-centroids.mjs`](../scripts/gen-centroids.mjs).

| Entries | Source |
|---|---|
| 171 countries | bbox centre of the largest landmass in [`public/world-map.json`](../public/world-map.json) |
| 29 microstates and small island states | capital coordinates, hand-written in the generator — the world map carries no feature for them |

The values are approximate by design: they place a country, they are not survey
data. Whatever license covers `public/world-map.json` also covers the 171
entries derived from it.
